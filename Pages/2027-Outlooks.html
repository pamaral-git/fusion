Three Core Macro Fault Lines DEBATE
1. US Resilience vs. Fiscal Hangover

Cautious: Barclays flags negative real wage growth slowing consumption; Columbia Threadneedle warns of a 2027 fiscal hangover.
Constructive: ABN AMRO sees defence capex picking up the baton; J.P. Morgan and Invesco expect AI capex & cooling oil to keep expansion intact.

2. Europe: Structural Bruise vs. Rollout Upside

Bearish/Cautious: ABN AMRO expects below-trend eurozone growth; ING flags France at 0.5% GDP and 5.5% deficit; Goldman sees EUR downside until France's April election.
Constructive: Lily Gilmour favors European equities on German stimulus & AI rollout; Santander sees Eurozone accelerating to 1.4%.

3. Inflation & Central Bank Cuts

Higher-for-Longer: Barclays expects Fed hikes around turn of year then a long pause; Santander sees upward repricing of the neutral rate.
Disinflation Pivot: ABN AMRO and Goldman see Middle East supply recovery & fading tariff/energy shocks enabling late-2027 rate cuts.
